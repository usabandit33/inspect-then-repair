A Dallas fire protection visit is two jobs. Name the annual inspection vs the separate repair of deficiencies before you sign.

Owners mix a scheduled annual NFPA inspection with a repair quote for deficiencies the inspector finds.

Ask for the written inspection report first. Ask whether the inspection price includes any repairs or if deficiencies are quoted separately.

  • Ask whether this visit is the annual inspection (NFPA 72 alarm or NFPA 25 sprinkler) or a repair of deficiencies.
  • Ask for the written report with device-level notes before any repair work.
  • Ask whether the inspection price includes repairs or if deficiencies are quoted separately after the report.
  • Ask whether the company files the report with the local fire marshal / AHJ.
  • Ask for company and technician licenses that will appear on the report.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
