Common questions

Is an annual fire alarm or sprinkler inspection the same as a repair?

No. An annual inspection tests and documents the system per NFPA 72 or NFPA 25. Repairs of deficiencies are separate work. Ask which is quoted.

What should the inspection report include?

Device-level notes, pass/fail, and any deficiencies. Ask for the report before approving repairs.

Who licenses fire protection companies in Texas?

Texas requires specific licenses for fire alarm and sprinkler work. Ask for the company and technician licenses. This site is not a licensing board.

Why is a repair quote higher than the annual inspection?

Inspection is testing and documentation. Parts and labor for repairs are different. Ask for the report first. This site does not set prices.
